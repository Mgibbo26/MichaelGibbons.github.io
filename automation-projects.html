Media Invoice Reconciliation Engine

Problem: Vendor invoice PDFs arrive through shared mailboxes and must be read, filed, checked against media billing detail, allocated and routed — with every held amount or Media Account Query tracked to closure.

01
  1. Receive & manifestScan the shared mailbox; save PDFs and original messages to an immutable run manifest.
  2. Read & fileDerive Year / Month / Agency / Vendor from invoice content rather than email subject lines.
  3. ClassifySeparate invoices, remittances and unknown documents; quarantine unsupported files.
  4. ExtractCapture invoice header and line detail; use OCR fallback and verify line roll-up to subtotal.
  5. Load billingLoad MISR, non-financial and unbilled sources into a controlled reconciliation store.
  6. MapApply approved vendor, agency and placement crosswalks; hold new mappings for approval.
  7. MatchRun exact and cascading logic across invoice number, date, placement and amount; flag ambiguous matches.
  8. Allocate & decideAssign AP Ready, Held, Media Account Query, Pending Billing or Exception using a configurable tolerance.
  9. Draft action emailsCreate reviewable Outlook drafts with the original PDF and invoice-level reconciliation attached.
  10. Track to closePublish workbooks and dashboard; re-check open items on the next MISR refresh and retain the audit log.

Core

  • Python, Pandas and openpyxl
  • DuckDB reconciliation store
  • pdfplumber + OCR fallback

Experience

  • Streamlit review dashboard
  • Excel evidence packs
  • Outlook draft automation

Matching

  • Rule-based cascade
  • Date and amount tolerance
  • Fuzzy text only behind deterministic checks

Multi-Source Usage-to-Billing Control Engine

Problem: Monthly usage and delivery data arrives from multiple systems in inconsistent layouts. Finance must convert it into defensible billing, cost and margin — then reconcile the result to sender totals, CRM budgets and forecast.

Generalised from: a specialist multi-platform billing engine. Platform, agency and insertion-order language becomes source systems, customers and contract/order keys; the control architecture remains intact.
02
  1. Collect source packsSave monthly usage files, CRM extracts, manual adjustments and forecast into a dated inbound folder.
  2. Identify by schemaDetect each source using sheet names, headers and required fields — not filename alone.
  3. Validate the periodCheck service month, schema, mandatory columns, duplicate files and expected row-count range.
  4. Load raw, unchangedWrite each source to a labelled raw table and record file hash, rows and control totals in the run log.
  5. Create canonical keysDerive contract/order/customer keys and map raw names to governed master data.
  6. Apply commercial rulesUse visible fee, rate, adjustment and inclusion tables; flag missing rules rather than guessing.
  7. Calculate billing & marginGenerate transaction-level billing, direct cost and contribution margin with full lineage.
  8. Reconcile & testCompare with sender totals, CRM budget and forecast; run 31 staged pass/fail checks.
  9. Publish & actionCreate billing, journal, variance dashboard and reviewable commentary drafts; archive the signed run.

Core

  • Python and openpyxl
  • Pandas data validation
  • Formula-driven Excel model

Integration

  • Email or monitored folder intake
  • CRM / ERP CSV extracts
  • Outlook draft generation

Outputs

  • Billing and cost ledger
  • GL journal
  • Budget / forecast variance pack

Revenue Actuals vs Forecast Consolidation + Web App

Problem: Actuals, forecast versions and operational job data sit in separate workbooks with inconsistent keys and time logic. Management needs one trusted view of performance, drivers and actions.

Generalised from: RevRec vs Forecast Consolidation + Web App. “Revenue recognition” becomes the wider actuals-versus-plan pattern, usable across business units and industries.
03
  1. Ingest governed versionsLoad actuals, forecast and job/order extracts with source filename, version and as-of date.
  2. Standardise dimensionsNormalise calendar, business unit, customer, job/order, status and currency fields.
  3. Validate sourcesCheck required columns, duplicate keys, row counts, date coverage and source totals.
  4. Build master tablesCreate a transaction fact table plus customer, job and forecast dimensions.
  5. Apply time logicUse selected reporting year, forecast version and last closed month to separate actual, forecast and pipeline.
  6. Calculate performanceProduce MTD/YTD actual, forecast, variance, trend and pipeline measures.
  7. Explain driversRank material variances and connect them to customer, job, timing, scope or rate drivers.
  8. Publish the appServe role-appropriate dashboards, filters, drill-downs and searchable job registers.
  9. Close the loopCapture commentary, export the management pack and complete source-to-dashboard reconciliation.

Credit & Accounts Receivable Resolution Engine

Problem: Aged credits cannot be resolved safely from an ageing report alone. Teams need to combine open-item, receipt, allocation and ledger evidence before deciding whether to clear, allocate, refund or investigate.

Generalised from: a credit-note and accounts-receivable reconciliation. The public version removes organisation-specific details and focuses on a universal evidence-and-approval workflow.
04
  1. Assemble evidenceLoad the credit population, current AR ageing, customer receipts, allocation history and relevant GL extracts.
  2. Normalise recordsStandardise customer IDs, document references, dates, currencies and signs; retain original values.
  3. Tie control totalsConfirm item counts, balances and as-of dates before any decision analysis.
  4. Match documentsUse exact references first, then approved amount/date/customer proximity; expose ambiguous matches.
  5. Assess account positionCalculate open debits, credits, net position and ageing by customer and document.
  6. Classify the pathRecommend Clear Matched Offset, Allocate to Invoice, Refund Candidate, Investigate or No Longer Open.
  7. Score evidenceRecord what proves each outcome and what remains missing, such as payment or allocation evidence.
  8. Create action queuePrioritise by value, age and confidence; assign owner, due date, approval and next action.
  9. Rerun & retainRefresh against the next AR extract, close resolved items and preserve the full decision pack.

Core

  • Python / Pandas
  • Excel and PDF ingestion
  • openpyxl evidence workbook

Decision layer

  • Deterministic matching rules
  • Account net-position logic
  • Evidence confidence flags

Outputs

  • Evidence and approval checklist
